Good to know

A few questions,
before you begin.

A little clarity makes planning your visit easier. For details specific to your garment or style, call the boutique.

What can I enquire about?+

Our business card identifies designer salwar suits, kurtis and sarees. For tailoring requirements or a particular style, call the boutique and ask what is currently available.

Do the pictures show current stock?+

No. The boutique and business-card photographs are earlier images. The category artworks are generated illustrations. Please speak with the boutique about current products before visiting.

How do I find out the price or delivery time?+

Prices, fabric availability and delivery dates depend on your enquiry. Call +91 98748 52284 to discuss your requirements directly; this website does not list unverified prices or turnaround promises.

Should I call before visiting?+

Yes. Call to confirm opening hours and any arrangements needed for a visit to 391/128, Prince Anwar Shah Road, Jadavpur, Kolkata.

Can I book or buy through this website?+

This website is an enquiry guide. Use the telephone links to contact the boutique. It does not collect payments or submit orders online.
